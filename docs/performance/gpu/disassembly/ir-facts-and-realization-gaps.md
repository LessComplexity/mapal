Source digests are in `IR-FACT-MAP-source-seals.json`; frozen PTX/resource evidence is in `static-facts.json`, `B-RESULTS.md` and `EXECUTED-IMAGES.md`. Semble was invoked once for this task but produced no result after several minutes and was terminated; root authorized known-path/degraded literal reads. The supported answer for the currently measured shapes is therefore: the IR already supplies shape, affine layout, operation order, independent outputs and substantial storage/fusion legality. There are concrete GPU realization restrictions and a geometry/resource policy whose measured choices can be suboptimal. Which additional hardware facts or realizations would close the cuBLAS gap remains an experiment until actual library SASS/counters and controlled alternatives discriminate the cause. The pending NCU diagnostic does not promote an algorithm automatically.
