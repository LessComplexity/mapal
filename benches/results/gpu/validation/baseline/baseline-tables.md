# S54 baseline: five architectures, multi-computation probes, library chains

Measured 2026-10-21 on vast.ai, one box per session, 28 rentals (every one destroyed). Ratios are library ms / Mapal ms (>1 = Mapal faster); medians.

## Headline

**Correctness.** The differential (GPU stdout vs the interpreter oracle, every program × arm × face) passed 238/238 on the RTX 3090 and the A100, and 246/246 on the RTX 4090, H100 PCIe and RTX 5090. Trap-before-site passed 10/10 on each part. Every later session passed 10–14 of 10–14. Every exact and contract kernel bench is bit-exact against the in-order fold, and every TF32 bench is within its bound. Every library leg's output is gated against Mapal's values, and all of them pass.

- **Whole chain:** Mapal is 1.5–42× slower than the best library chain on every probe and part, and 70–200× slower on `ew`.
- **Kernels alone** (contract face) are 0.62–1.39× the best library leg.
- **Where the gap comes from:** one launch per tile site, a whole d2h of every output that has a host reader, and every per-cell consumer running as a host loop. Independent sites run serially. `ew` has no device code. The only fusion is `pro`, the closed-form prologue composed by devgen.
- **The libraries' best legs:** the cuBLASLt epilogue (`epi`, `bias`, `pro`, `residual`, `mlp`, `loop`), two streams (`indep`, `fanout`, `diamond`, `mlp2` on some parts), the two-stream cuBLASLt (`mlp2`), sgemv (`rowsum`) and one fused kernel (`ew`).
- **Caveat:** Mapal's numbers are its own `iter ms` and the MAPAL_GPU_TRACE kernel time on host-generated data. The library legs are cudaEvent times on device-built data after a 256 MB L2 flush.

### Rung table changes (`GPU_RUNGS_OFF`, `crates/backends/llvm/src/profile.rs`)

- **RTX 3090** (new; sessions base + confirm, then verify + verify2):
  - OFF: `async` everywhere; under-filled `affine`, `interleave`, `warp`, `wide`, and `min-blocks` on exact; `idx32` on exact and under-filled contract; `ldmatrix` and `min-blocks` on contract; `wide-fma` filled exact; `retain-delta`.
  - `wide` ships ON filled. In verify, filled TF32 with `wide` off ran at 0.955 / 0.967 of the base build (2048 / 4096); verify2 measured it ON.
- **A100** (new; base on an A100 PCIe 40GB, confirm and verify1–3 on SXM4):
  - OFF: `async`, `idx32`, `warp` everywhere; `affine` and `unroll` on exact; `min-blocks` and `wide-fma` filled; under-filled `interleave` and TF32 `wide`; `retain-delta`.
  - `min-blocks` stays ON under-filled: +11 / +8% (contract / exact) in two sessions.
- **H100 PCIe:** `pad` ON under-filled, both faces. At 1024, two sessions measured +3.7 / +3.1% exact and +3.5 / +3.3% contract. The verify session measured +4.0% exact and +3.2% contract against the base build.
- **RTX 5090:** `wide` OFF filled TF32. OFF was faster at 2048 by 1.9% and 1.8% (base, confirm) and by 0.9% in verify. Verify measured ON 1.3% faster at 8192.
- **RTX 4090:** no change. The confirm session's contradictions all change sign between sessions.
- **Retain-delta OFF** holds on every part:
  - RTX 4090 confirm measured the rung ON at 0.807 / 0.789 s vs OFF 0.679 / 0.679 s.
  - On Ampere, resident beats retain-delta by 1.4–1.8× on decode.

### Open (measured, not shipped: one session, or the sign changes)

- **RTX 3090 around its new table** (verify + verify2):
  - `warp-fill` under-filled contract runs faster OFF: 0.940 / 0.959 on FMA and 0.876 / 0.887 on TF32, disjoint ranges in both sessions.
  - With register staging, `min-blocks` pays: +9% under-filled exact and +5–9% filled FMA. Under-filled TF32 it costs 11%.
  - `unroll` under-filled contract runs faster OFF (0.94 / 0.96).
- **A100 `idx32` and `wide-fma` filled contract:** the sign changes across the three verify sessions. `wide-fma` measured +2–5%, then −1–2%, then +1–2%. `idx32` measured mixed, then −1–3%, then +1–2%.
- **5090 / H100 "no effect" rungs that ship ON** (rung tables below, status `contradicts`): no size above ±1% in either session.
- **`devgen` on the probes** (base session only; arm iter / shipped iter, exact face): A100 `mlp2` 0.78 and `diamond` 0.80 at 4096, `loop` 0.90 at 4096; H100 `bias` 0.90 and `epi` 0.94 at 2048. On `e2e_4096` and `e2e_chain_4096` it pays +2–19% of process wall in every session.

Sessions: `base-<part>.txt` = the base build (the S53 table; Ampere every rung ON), every arm, the probes and the library chains; `-confirm.txt` = the same build's kernels, decode and e2e on a different box; `-verify*.txt` = the S54 table's kernels (after the rows below), next to the base build's. `-prof.txt` = ptxas, SASS and nsys evidence. Medians; `~` = the min–max ranges overlap. Per-part detail: `python3 benches/gpu/table.py base-<part>.txt`; rung decisions: `python3 benches/gpu/rungs.py <sessions>`.
