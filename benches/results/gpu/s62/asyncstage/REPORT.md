## Open

- asyncstage `(h100-pcie, Filled, Exact)`: the only cell is 16x2048x7168. It shows s 1.461/1.457 (round 1), 1.439/1.438 flushed and 1.365/1.327 warm (round 2, SXM), and 1.268 flushed and 1.256 warm (round 2, PCIe, one pass), with no stable loss. It is still held OFF. In the composed programs, `iter` loses on every part while the traced kernels do not, so the loss comes with the launch, not with the fill class. No composed program with a Filled asyncstage site was measured.
- H100 round 2 is partial: two boxes lost ssh mid-run, and the residual-MLP probe for H100 is from round 1 only.
