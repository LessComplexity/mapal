# S54 `epilogue` (R1): per-cell consumers in the tile site's store, five parts

Measured 2026-10-21 on vast.ai: one box per part, plus an H100 PCIe confirm on a different host.
Every instance was destroyed. Ratios are medians of 5 reps. A ratio above 1 means ON is faster,
or Mapal is faster. Raw data: `epilogue-<part>.txt`. Tables: `python3 benches/gpu/probe_arms.py
[--vs-base] epilogue-*.txt`. Static counts: `epilogue-fusion-report.txt`. Registers:
`epilogue-ptxas.txt`.

## Kernel registers (ptxas -v, n = 4096; `epilogue-ptxas.txt`)

Registers move by −10 to +8 per kernel, and no epilogue kernel spills more than its OFF kernel.
Residency changes only where a count crosses 128 with 256-thread blocks. On the H100's exact face, `epi` uses 132 registers ON against 128 OFF, so one
256-thread block fits per SM instead of two; its kernel is 13–21% slower ON in both sessions. On
the A100's contract face, `bias` uses 129 against 128 + 20 B of spill, with no measured kernel
cost. On the RTX 5090's exact face, the epilogue kernels spill 8 B where the OFF kernel spills
20 B.

## Effect: ON vs `off-epilogue`, Mapal's own clock, 9 probes × n = 2048, 4096 × both faces

Ships ON on every part. Every median is at least 1.10 except one row, and every ratio of minima
is at least 1.29, so no further confirm was needed. The exception is H100 PCIe `indep` 2048,
contract, at 0.26. Three of its five ON reps hit 20–59 ms host stalls; that box's processes ran
0.9–1.5 s against about 0.3 s elsewhere. Its minima give 2.38, and the confirm box gives 20.44. Only
the filled class (n ≥ 2048) was measured, so `GPU_RUNGS_OFF` has no row.

## Against the libraries

Mapal still loses the whole-program comparison on every probe and part, on both faces. At
n = 4096 on the contract face, the eight straight-line probes went from 0.10–0.50 of the best
library leg to 0.44–0.94; `loop` went from 0.05–0.14 to 0.07–0.42. Two costs remain inside
Mapal's clock:

- `cuMemFree` of the dead operands and the output's `cuMemAlloc`: `free_ms` is about 2.1 ms per
  traced run at 4096 on the RTX 4090. This is plan item 6, `arena`.
- In `loop`, the host's copies of the carried state product: 3 × 64 MB `memcpy` per step at 4096,
  of a host slot the device carry never writes. Also item 6.

On kernel time alone (contract), Mapal matches or beats the best library leg on the RTX 3090
(0.89–1.09), RTX 4090 (0.88–1.08), H100 PCIe (0.71–1.07) and RTX 5090 (0.81–1.04). The A100
reaches 0.67–0.94. The exact face is 0.33–0.67 everywhere (two instructions per MAC).
