decode: +2 kernels (the one-row K/V kernel, s = q·Kᵀ's), the 1 MB d2h of K per step is gone (K and
V never leave the device; s runs there), the 4 KB h2d of s is gone; what crosses per step is x's
written row and q (in), o (out). loop_rewrite (the column-write control): s moves to the device
(lane-stride), nothing is retained or delta-uploaded. px_loop_256, loop_128_8, loop_mm_1024_8
(site-produced carries, Δ = Top): unchanged.

decode_16_8, ON vs s54-base: bytes in 9216 → 1504 (x: one whole upload, then its 32-byte row),
bytes out 8704 → 512 (K's 512 B whole copy per step gone; o's 32 B stays), launches 3 → 4 per step
(s joins the device). Per step after the first: 2 h2d (x's row, q), 4 launches (K row, V row, s, o),
1 d2h (o). retain-resident does not change these counts: it changes the K and V launches from the
whole site to one row (O(T·d²) → O(d²) work per step), which only the box measures.

The emulator caught one real bug before any box ran: the strided site on the contract face first
emitted `llvm.fma`, while the CPU (which does not tile it) keeps the map's `fmul` + `fadd`; the
contract-face oracle differed in the last digits. Fixed: a strided site keeps the host step
(`gpu.rs:kernel_text`, `contract && b.clane == 1`).
